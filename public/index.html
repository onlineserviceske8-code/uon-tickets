University of Nairobi Freshers Night

Sat 18 Sept, 6:00 pm KICC, Nairobi 6:00 PM

About This Event

The University of Nairobi Students' Association (UNSA) proudly presents Freshers Night 2026 — the biggest welcome celebration for UoN's incoming class, live at the iconic Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC) on 18th September 2026, from 6:00 PM onwards.

This is more than a party — it's the official kickoff to campus life. Whether you're stepping onto UoN grounds for the very first time or you're a returning student ready to show the freshers what university nights are really about, this is where new friendships are made, new memories are created, and your university journey truly begins.

Expect a high-energy night packed with music, entertainment, surprise performances, and the kind of atmosphere only thousands of students in one room can create. From the moment you walk in, it's all about good music, great company, and celebrating the start of something new.

Event Highlights:

  • Live DJs and entertainment all night
  • Meet freshers and students from across all UoN campuses
  • Held at KICC — Nairobi's premier event venue
  • Officially ticketed by MADFUN for a safe, organized experience
